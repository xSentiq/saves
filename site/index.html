Import old DMs

This pulls every file, image, video and link out of your old DMs, using Discord's official data export.

  1. In Discord, go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Request all of my data. Tick Messages and send the request.
  2. Discord emails you a package.zip, usually within a few days.
  3. Pick that zip below. It's read in your browser; only the links and files from the DM you choose get sent to the vault.

The export only has your own messages, so you and furfyl each import your own package. Things you saved before are skipped automatically.
